Origins & Creative Liberties

Not Another DarkRP Clone: Rebuilt from the Ground Up

In 2013, mercior created GangWars RP, establishing a gritty urban crime setting where player gangs fought street-by-street for territorial supremacy.

Juicebox, who served as a developer and senior administrator on GangWars RP back in the day, built GridRP as a ground-up reimagining inspired by that classic foundation. Rather than an imitation, GridRP takes substantial creative liberties and introduces deep custom systems of its own.

GridRP is explicitly not another DarkRP clone. Running on the clean Garry's Mod sandbox base rather than recycled DarkRP code, it replaces passive printer-sitting and generic jobs with an active Gang Stock Exchange, physical L3 network switch cabling, Metasploit cyber warfare, and permanent player progression across sessions.

Base Gamemode: sandbox (Not DarkRP)
Inspiration: GangWars RP (2013 by mercior)

How GridRP Differs from DarkRP
  • ✗ Passive printer sitting in closed rooms
